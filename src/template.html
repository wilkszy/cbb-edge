CBB Edge: Book vs. KenPom

Top 5 plays: most likely to hit

The five bets with the highest win probability today, across spreads, totals, team totals and moneylines, one per game. Probabilities come from the KenPom projection, pulled halfway toward the market's 50/50 (moneylines use the 50/50 model/market blend). A play only qualifies if its probability beats the break-even rate for its price. Moneylines shorter than −300 are skipped.

Spread value: competitive games

Spreads of 20 or less where the KenPom projection and the book differ by 3+ points. Biggest gaps first.

Spread value: big spreads

Spreads over 20 where the projection and the book differ by 3+ points. Almost always the underdog. Tracked separately in Track record.

Best moneyline value

Model win probability (KenPom blended 50/50 with the market) minus the market's no-vig probability. Shown when the edge is at least 3 points and the market gives the side a 15% to 85% chance.

Spread and moneyline agree

Games where both the spread gap (2+ pts) and the moneyline edge point to the same side.
